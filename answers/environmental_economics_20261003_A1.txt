[HEADING] A.1

Sustainable development means development that meets the needs of the present generation without reducing the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

It tries to maintain a balance between economic growth, social welfare and protection of the environment. Development should therefore use natural resources carefully and avoid unnecessary environmental damage.

Important features of sustainable development are:

1. Efficient use of natural resources.

2. Protection of forests, water, soil and biodiversity.

3. Reduction of pollution and waste.

4. Use of renewable sources of energy.

5. Consideration of the needs of future generations.

Thus, sustainable development aims at improving living standards while protecting the environment for the future.
