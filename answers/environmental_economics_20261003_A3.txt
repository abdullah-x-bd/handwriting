Five important inter-state water disputes in India are:

1. Cauvery Water Dispute between Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Puducherry.

2. Krishna Water Dispute involving Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

3. Ravi-Beas Water Dispute involving Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan.

4. Mahadayi or Mandovi Water Dispute involving Goa, Karnataka and Maharashtra.

5. Mahanadi Water Dispute mainly between Odisha and Chhattisgarh.

These disputes mainly arise because different states depend on the same river for irrigation, drinking water, electricity generation and other purposes. Differences over the sharing and use of river water often lead to conflicts between states.
